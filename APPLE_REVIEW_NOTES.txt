1) PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
"Sehhati Alnafsiyah" (صحتي النفسية) connects patients with licensed psychotherapists for private, scheduled video sessions. Problem solved: finding a verified therapist and booking a private session without leaving the phone. Audience: adults seeking psychological support (patients) and licensed therapists (providers). The app is NOT an emergency service and does not give diagnoses; this is stated in the app and store listing.

2) HOW TO ACCESS (review login, no Apple ID needed)
Public users sign in with Sign in with Apple (or Google). For review:
a. On the welcome screen choose the tab "مريض" (Patient) or "معالج" (Therapist), tap "ابدأ".
b. On the sign-in screen tap the small grey link "دخول المراجعين (للمتاجر)" at the bottom (or tap the lotus logo 5 times); the dialog "دخول المراجعة" opens.
c. Enter reviewer@wardyat.net / uf8JFp7QPawstYNu, tap "دخول".
Patient: tab "المعالجون" > pick the review therapist > pick a slot > "احجز الجلسة" > "جلساتي". Past sessions appear under "الجلسات السابقة".
Therapist: manage available slots, sessions and private notes, profile and license.
Video sessions open 10 minutes before the appointment (camera + microphone permission needed). Sessions are NOT recorded or stored.
ACCOUNT DELETION: "حسابي" > "حذف الحساب" deletes the account and its data (also described at https://nafsiyati.wardyat.net/support/).
USER-GENERATED CONTENT: none is shared between users (no feed/chat/public profiles). Therapist notes are private to the therapist. No reporting/blocking mechanism is therefore needed.
PAID CONTENT: no in-app purchases. Session fees are settled outside the app (manual confirmation by the platform admin); no payment processor is integrated.

3) EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Own server (nafsiyati.wardyat.net, Node.js) for accounts, therapists, bookings.
- wardyat-core (core.wardyat.net) for authentication via Sign in with Apple / Google.
- LiveKit (self-hosted, lk.wardyat.net) for WebRTC video.
No AI services, no analytics/ad SDKs, no tracking.

4) REGIONS
The app functions identically in all regions. Therapist profiles list the therapist's country (initial pilot: Algeria, with Tunisia and Saudi Arabia supported). UI is Arabic (RTL).

5) REGULATED INDUSTRY
Mental-health services. Every therapist must submit a license number and a license certificate image; the admin reviews and verifies it before the therapist becomes visible to patients. The platform does not employ therapists and does not provide medical advice itself. No protected third-party material.
